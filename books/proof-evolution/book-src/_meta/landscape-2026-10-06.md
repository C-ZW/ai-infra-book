# Evidence baseline — 2026-10-06

Historical dates distinguish composition, surviving witness, edition, translation and later reconstruction. Chapter-specific footnotes are the canonical citations; this file records high-risk editorial boundaries and the dated frontier snapshot.

## Historical and mathematical boundaries

- Do not infer absence of proof from an algorithmic genre or from missing surviving documents.
- Do not assign a unique inventor of proof, algebra, induction, calculus, or scientific method.
- Euclid's text is not a fully formal modern axiom system. Product-plus-one in the prime proof need not be prime.
- Relative consistency of non-Euclidean geometry depends on the background construction of the model; a sphere is not a model preserving every other Euclidean axiom.
- First-order logical completeness and incompleteness of sufficiently strong effective arithmetic theories concern different objects.
- Gödel's hypotheses, the chosen consistency sentence, and the distinction between truth in a model and derivability must remain explicit.
- A mathematical proof and empirical support have different forms of residual uncertainty. Statistical evidence is model- and design-dependent.
- Reproducibility/replicability terminology varies by field; name the adopted convention and its source.
- Formal verification checks a formal proposition under dependencies and assumptions; it does not itself validate translation, physical measurements, or hardware.

## Contemporary snapshot protocol

Confirm each benchmark claim in a primary research paper, official project repository, or official result account. Record task set, assistance, time constraints, scoring and verification type. Distinguish the paper's publication date from the event date. Sources later than the cutoff are excluded.
