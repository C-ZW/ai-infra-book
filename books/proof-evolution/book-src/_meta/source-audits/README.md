# Author research records

These five JSON files preserve the chapter authors' actual source work, numerical checks, scope decisions and access limits. They support provenance and future maintenance. They are author notes, not independent reviewer verdicts; the separate reports live in `../reviews/`.

Some sources were read as full text, others as a specific section, original-language passage, institutional catalogue, abstract or indexed excerpt. A citation in a chapter does not imply that every page of the underlying work was inspected. Each record preserves the applicable limits. The book itself identifies modern reconstructions and hypothetical examples rather than presenting them as historical documents or empirical observations.

Conversation-only retrieval IDs are omitted from the exported records. Accessible source URLs and descriptions of what was actually read are retained. Final text and uniform character counts are fixed by `../source-snapshot.json`; an author's local counting convention may produce a slightly different number.
